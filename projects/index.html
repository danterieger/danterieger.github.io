    7. Wind turbine tower vibration absorber

      Team design project, Winter 2024

      A lumped-mass model of an 85 m tower showed a resonance inside the 4 to 6 Hz wind band, so we designed a spring-mounted absorber with compression springs and EPDM dampers. A later re-check found a simulation bug and worked out the correctly tuned absorber.
